## Core thesis

## Summary

The closure of the Strait of Hormuz has choked Gulf crude and jet‑fuel exports, sending prices soaring. U.S. low‑cost carrier Spirit Airlines folded on May 2, and major airlines in Europe and Asia have cut flights, added surcharges, or face stock shortages. While the U.S. can increase exports to Europe, Asian markets remain vulnerable. Forecasts show a modest summer schedule rise despite the supply crunch, creating a disconnect between demand and fuel availability.
