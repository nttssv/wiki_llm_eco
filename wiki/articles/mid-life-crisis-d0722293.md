# Mid-life crisis

## Core thesis

Millennial brands are struggling to maintain their market positions due to rising competition and changing consumer acquisition costs.

## Summary

Millennial brands like Allbirds and Glossier are struggling as competition increases and customer acquisition costs rise. Allbirds plans to pivot to AI infrastructure after losing 99% of its market value since its peak. Other brands face similar challenges, with many unable to sustain their initial growth.
