# Kulturkampf

## Core thesis

UniCredit aims to combine its HVB subsidiary with Commerzbank to control ~9% of German banking revenue, using a low‑price bid to force a merger.

## Summary

UniCredit offered an 8% discount to acquire the remaining shares of Commerzbank, seeking to merge its HVB subsidiary and gain a 9% market share in Germany. The bid faces pushback from Commerzbank's management, the German government, and regulator BaFin, while UniCredit's CEO Andrea Orcel signals possible offer adjustments.
