# Blues on the Bay

## Core thesis

While AI companies are generating immense wealth, San Francisco's economy is not benefiting equally, leading to a potential crisis.

## Summary

Despite being the hub of AI innovation, San Francisco faces economic challenges, including a struggling housing market and declining employment in tech. The city’s wealth from AI is not translating into broader economic stability, with many residents leaving and a significant budget deficit looming.
