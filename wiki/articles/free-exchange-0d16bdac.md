# Free Exchange

## Core thesis

The concentration of oil revenues in dollars since the 1970s has underpinned the United States' financial and geopolitical power.

## Summary

The article traces the origin of the "petrodollar" concept, its role in cementing US dollar dominance through oil pricing and secret Treasury bond purchases, and examines how recent geopolitical shifts—especially the Iran war and China's push for yuan‑based oil payments—are challenging that regime, while also introducing the broader idea of "hostage capital" as a risk to the dollar's future.

## Narrative shift

- **Petrodollar as pillar of US dollar dominance** (emerging): The concentration of oil revenues in dollars since the 1970s has underpinned the United States' financial and geopolitical power.
