# Nothing in the tank

## Core thesis

The closure of the Strait of Hormuz is leading to unprecedented oil supply shortages that will have severe global repercussions.

## Summary

The ongoing crisis in oil markets, exacerbated by the closure of the Strait of Hormuz due to conflict, is leading to significant supply shortages and rising prices. Despite initial market resilience, the situation is expected to worsen, with potential severe consequences for global economies as demand destruction and stock depletion continue.

## Key entities

- **Iran** (Country): Actor. Involved in the closure of the Strait of Hormuz and retaliatory actions against commercial vessels.
- **United States** (Country): Actor. Engaged in military actions against Iran and affected by rising oil prices.
- **Saudi Arabia** (Country): Producer. Holds surplus oil capacity that is currently inaccessible due to the Strait's closure.
- **OPEC** (Organization): Regulator. Influences global oil production and pricing.
- **International Energy Agency (IEA)** (Organization): Regulator. Coordinated a historic drawdown of strategic oil reserves among member countries.
