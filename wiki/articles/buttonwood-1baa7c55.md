# Buttonwood

## Core thesis

The divergence in banking regulations between the U.S. and Europe could lead to a competitive disadvantage for European banks and increased financial instability.

## Summary

The global banking system faces a potential crisis as the U.S. and Europe diverge on capital requirements, risking a competitive disadvantage for European banks. Recent U.S. regulatory changes could lead to increased leverage in the banking sector, while European regulators respond with their own adjustments. This trend towards deregulation may heighten the risk of financial instability.
