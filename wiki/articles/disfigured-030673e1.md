# Disfigured

## Core thesis

Investments in statistical agencies yield high economic returns; attacks on data integrity can measurably depress GDP and employment.

## Summary

A working paper by Nicholas Bloom and co‑authors quantifies the economic damage from undermining U.S. statistical agencies, estimating that the August 2025 firing of BLS commissioner Erika McEntarfer raised policy uncertainty and cut GDP by $20‑$100 billion. The study highlights that each dollar spent on the BLS yields about $25 in economic benefit, underscoring the tangible value of reliable data for investors and policymakers.
