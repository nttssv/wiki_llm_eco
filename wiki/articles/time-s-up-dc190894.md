## Core thesis

The closure of the Strait of Hormuz and ongoing supply disruptions are leading the world towards an energy crisis.

## Summary

The ongoing Iran war has severely disrupted global energy markets, leading to significant oil supply shortages and rising prices. With the Strait of Hormuz closed, the world faces a potential energy catastrophe as demand remains high and inventories dwindle. The situation is exacerbated by government interventions in Europe and Asia, which may lead to further market imbalances.
