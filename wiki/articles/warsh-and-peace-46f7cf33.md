# Warsh and peace

## Core thesis

Kevin Warsh's ability to navigate political pressures will define his legacy at the Fed.

## Summary

Kevin Warsh is poised to become the next chairman of the Federal Reserve following the withdrawal of a criminal investigation into Jerome Powell. His ability to maintain the Fed's independence amidst political pressure from President Trump will be crucial, especially in light of rising inflation and oil prices. Warsh's proposed reforms include reducing the Fed's balance sheet and focusing on a broader range of data.
