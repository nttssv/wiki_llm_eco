# The salesman in the machine

## Core thesis

AI chatbots are transitioning into advertising platforms, changing the landscape of digital marketing.

## Summary

AI chatbots are evolving into sales tools, integrating advertisements into user conversations. This shift poses significant implications for both the AI and advertising industries, as companies like OpenAI and Google explore monetization strategies through ads in chat interactions. Early experiments show varied approaches and user responses, with potential for rapid growth in ad revenue.
