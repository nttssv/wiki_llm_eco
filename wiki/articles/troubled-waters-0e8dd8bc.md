# Troubled waters

## Core thesis

Confined environments like cruise ships can facilitate rare human‑to‑human hantavirus spread, prompting coordinated international containment efforts.

## Summary

A hantavirus outbreak aboard the MV Hondius, sailing from Argentina to Cape Verde, has resulted in three confirmed deaths and several suspected cases. The WHO is leading contact tracing and sequencing, while Spain plans to quarantine remaining passengers at the Canary Islands. The Andes strain involved can spread between humans, but global risk remains low.
