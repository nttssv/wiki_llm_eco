# Free Exchange

## Core thesis

The World Bank's new report indicates a potential shift towards supporting industrial policy, challenging previous doctrines.

## Summary

The World Bank's new report, 'Industrial Policy for Development', suggests a shift towards endorsing government intervention in industrial policy, challenging the long-held Washington Consensus. While it acknowledges the need for public inputs like infrastructure, it remains cautious about market incentives, warning against the risks of interventionism.
