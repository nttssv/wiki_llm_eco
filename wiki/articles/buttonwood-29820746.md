# Buttonwood

## Core thesis

The stablecoin market has not achieved the expected growth due to regulatory uncertainties and low demand.

## Summary

The stablecoin market has stagnated after initial growth, facing regulatory challenges and soft demand. Despite predictions of significant growth, the market's value has remained stable, raising concerns among banks about potential impacts on deposits and lending.
