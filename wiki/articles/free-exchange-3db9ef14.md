# Free Exchange

## Core thesis

Exporting people can be a viable economic strategy for growth, but its success is contingent on external factors.

## Summary

The article explores how Kerala's economy has benefited from remittances due to emigration to the Gulf, contrasting this with the challenges of relying on such a model for broader economic growth amidst global uncertainties.

## Key entities

- **Kerala** (Location): Economic case study. A southern Indian state that has prospered through remittances from emigrants working in the Gulf.
- **Gulf States** (Location): Destination for emigrants. Region where many Keralites work and send remittances back home.
- **World Bank** (Organization): Research entity. Provides estimates on remittances and their impact on national income.
