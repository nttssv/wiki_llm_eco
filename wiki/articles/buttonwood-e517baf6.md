## Core thesis

Bill Ackman's Pershing Square USA aims to eliminate persistent NAV discounts by leveraging a domestic listing, large manager stake, and strong branding, potentially restoring investor confidence in closed‑end funds.

## Summary

Bill Ackman raised $5 bn to launch Pershing Square USA, the largest US‑listed closed‑end fund, hoping to overcome chronic NAV discounts that plague the asset class. By listing domestically and retaining a large manager stake, he seeks to attract stable capital and replicate the outperformance of his earlier Pershing Square Holdings, while recent activist activity (Saba Capital vs. Edinburgh Worldwide) underscores the risks of discount‑driven takeovers.
