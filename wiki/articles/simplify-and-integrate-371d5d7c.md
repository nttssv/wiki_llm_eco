# Simplify and integrate

## Core thesis

The European Commission is launching a coordinated effort to cut red tape and harmonise rules, aiming to boost GDP and restore competitiveness.

## Summary

The EU is confronting a decade of sluggish growth by slashing bureaucracy and deepening market integration. New “omnibus” bills aim to cut firms’ reporting costs by €37.5 bn by 2029, while proposals such as services passports and a “28th regime” for pan‑European startups target a 3 % GDP boost over ten years. Political backing from von der Leyen and the finance commissioner is strong, but national resistance and recent negotiation breakdowns threaten implementation.
