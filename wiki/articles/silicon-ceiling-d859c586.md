# Silicon ceiling

## Core thesis

The rapid growth in AI demand is outpacing the supply chain's ability to provide necessary hardware.

## Summary

The AI industry is facing a significant supply-chain crunch as demand for computing power surges. Major AI firms are struggling to keep up, leading to service throttling and outages. Investments in infrastructure are increasing, but political opposition and insufficient hardware manufacturing threaten to exacerbate the situation.

## Key entities

- **Anthropic** (Company): AI lab. An AI lab that has begun throttling access to its tools due to high demand.
- **OpenAI** (Company): AI lab. A leading AI lab that has shut down its video-generation tool to redirect resources.

- **Google** (Company): Investor. Invested in Anthropic to help meet computing needs.

- **TSMC** (Company): Chip manufacturer. World's largest contract chipmaker facing tight supply conditions.
