# Soda wars

## Core thesis

Pepsi is attempting to recover market share by adopting strategies similar to Coca-Cola.

## Summary

Coca-Cola leads the soft-drink market with a 17% share, while Pepsi holds 11%. Pepsi's recent struggles stem from price increases and changing consumer preferences. An activist investor's involvement has prompted Pepsi to cut costs and streamline its product range, showing early signs of recovery.

## Key entities

- **Coca-Cola** (Company): Competitor. Leading soft-drink manufacturer with a 17% market share.
- **Pepsi** (Company): Competitor. Second-largest soft-drink manufacturer with an 11% market share.
- **Elliott Management** (Investor): Activist Investor. Hedge fund that has taken a $4bn stake in Pepsi and pushed for strategic changes.
- **Poppi** (Brand): Acquisition. Prebiotic-soda brand acquired by Pepsi for $2bn.
