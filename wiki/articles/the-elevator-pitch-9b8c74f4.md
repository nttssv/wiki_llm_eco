# The elevator pitch

## Core thesis

The merger between Kone and TKE will create a dominant player in the lift industry, reshaping market dynamics.

## Summary

Kone and TKE have merged to create the world's largest lift manufacturer, aiming for significant cost savings and expanded service capabilities. This merger positions them to dominate the market, despite challenges from competitors and potential regulatory scrutiny.
