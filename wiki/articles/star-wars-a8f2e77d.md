# Star wars

## Core thesis

Blue Origin is struggling to catch up with SpaceX in the space industry despite significant investments.

## Summary

Blue Origin's recent launch of the New Glenn rocket ended in failure as it placed its payload in the wrong orbit, leading to an investigation by the FAA. This setback comes as Amazon invests heavily in satellite internet services to compete with SpaceX's Starlink.

- **Blue Origin** (Company): Rocket manufacturer. A rocket firm owned by Jeff Bezos, focused on spaceflight and satellite services.
- **Jeff Bezos** (Person): Founder of Amazon and Blue Origin. Business magnate and investor, known for his role in the space industry.
