# Vanity project

## Core thesis

Estée Lauder hopes a $6 bn acquisition of Puig will restore growth and diversify geography, but the deal may distract from deeper operational issues.

## Summary

Estée Lauder, once valued at $133 bn, has fallen to $31 bn as Chinese daigou restrictions and underperforming acquisitions eroded growth. New CEO Stéphane de La Faverie is cutting jobs, expanding online, and pursuing a $6 bn takeover of Spain’s Puig to boost geography and perfume sales, while rival L’Oréal continues to outpace it with its own Kering beauty acquisition.
