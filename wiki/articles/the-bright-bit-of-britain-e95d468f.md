# The bright bit of Britain

## Core thesis

London's financial sector is recovering and expanding despite Brexit and economic challenges.

## Summary

Despite economic challenges, London's financial sector remains robust, showing resilience post-Brexit. Major banks are investing in new headquarters, and the City is regaining its competitive edge in global finance. The government is encouraged to support this growth through regulatory reforms and investment strategies.
