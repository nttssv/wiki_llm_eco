# Imported enthusiasm

## Core thesis

Social media endorsements are significantly influencing foreign perceptions of China, leading to increased tourism.

## Summary

The phenomenon of 'Chinamaxxing' is boosting China's tourism and economy, driven by foreign social media endorsements and a new visa-free entry program. While initial excitement has led to increased foreign visits and spending, the long-term sustainability of this trend remains uncertain.
