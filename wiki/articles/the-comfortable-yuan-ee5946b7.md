# The comfortable yuan

## Core thesis

The yuan is emerging as a viable alternative to the dollar, driven by geopolitical factors and China's financial infrastructure.

## Summary

China is increasingly promoting the yuan as a viable alternative to the US dollar for international transactions, aided by geopolitical tensions and the development of its payment infrastructure. The yuan's acceptance is growing, particularly in countries wary of US financial dominance, with significant increases in transaction volumes through China's payment systems.

## Key entities

- **Xi Jinping** (Person): Leader of China. Current President of China advocating for a stronger yuan.
- **CIPS** (System): Payment System. Cross-Border Interbank Payment System facilitating yuan transactions.
- **e-CNY** (Currency): Digital Currency. Digital currency issued by China's central bank.
- **Iran** (Country): Trade Partner. Country accepting yuan for oil exports.
- **SWIFT** (System): Bank Messaging Network. Global financial messaging service that facilitates international payments.
- **IMF** (Organization): International Monetary Fund. International organization discussing global financial stability.
- **Standard Chartered** (Bank): Financial Institution. Bank involved in facilitating yuan transactions.
