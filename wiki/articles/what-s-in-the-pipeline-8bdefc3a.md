# What’s in the pipeline

## Core thesis

The reconstruction of oil and gas infrastructure in the Middle East will create significant opportunities for oil-services firms.

## Summary

Oil-services firms are poised for growth due to post-war reconstruction opportunities in the Middle East and a potential increase in investment from oil majors. Despite short-term challenges from geopolitical tensions, the long-term outlook suggests a significant boom in the sector.
