# The Square Mile

## Core thesis

Despite fears post-Brexit, London's financial sector is recovering and regaining its competitive edge.

## Summary

The City of London has shown resilience post-Brexit, maintaining employment levels and economic output in the financial services sector. Despite challenges from other global financial hubs, London is regaining its competitive edge, driven by improved sentiment and regulatory support.
