# No respite for the rupee

## Core thesis

Despite the rupee's depreciation, India's economic growth potential remains strong.

## Summary

India's rupee has depreciated significantly, reflecting deeper economic issues and a struggle to attract foreign investment. The Reserve Bank of India has intervened to stabilize the currency amidst rising oil prices due to the Iran war, but challenges remain.
