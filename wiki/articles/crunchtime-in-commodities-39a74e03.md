# Crunchtime in commodities

## Core thesis

The war in Iran has caused unprecedented disruptions in oil supply, leading to soaring prices.

## Summary

The ongoing war in Iran has led to a significant oil supply shock, with Brent crude prices spiking above $125. Despite this, traders expect prices to fall, indicating a disconnect with the reality of supply shortages. The situation is complicated by geopolitical tensions and the potential for further disruptions in oil supply, particularly through the Strait of Hormuz.
