# Meet the peptideins

## Core thesis

The discovery and systematic naming of peptideins will open a previously hidden layer of the proteome, providing novel targets for immunotherapies and other drugs.

## Summary

A new class of tiny protein‑like molecules called peptideins has been mapped in the human genome, expanding the ‘dark proteome’. Researchers led by Sebastiaan van Heesch suggest they could become drug targets, especially for cancer immunotherapy, but warn that many may be false positives pending better validation.
